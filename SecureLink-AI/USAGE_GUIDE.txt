HOW THE BOT WORKS
-----------------
Send any suspicious URL to the bot on Telegram.
It checks it against VirusTotal, Google Safe Browsing,
URLScan.io, and an ML model trained on 235,000 real URLs.
You get a Risk Score (0–100) + plain-English explanation.

That's it. No commands needed. Just paste the URL.


----------------------------------------------------------------
RATE LIMITS — HOW MANY SCANS CAN YOU DO PER DAY?
----------------------------------------------------------------

The free APIs have daily quotas shared across ALL users:

  VirusTotal        → 500 scans/day  ← the tightest limit
  Google Safe Brwsg → 10,000/day     ← very comfortable
  URLScan.io        → 100 scans/day  ← moderate
  Bot (per user)    → 1 scan per 10 seconds (cooldown)

GOOD NEWS — The Cache System:
  If 5 friends scan the SAME URL, it only counts as
  1 VirusTotal call (not 5). Results are cached for 24 hours.
  So scanning popular sites repeatedly costs nothing.


PRACTICAL DAILY BUDGET (rough guide)
--------------------------------------
  Unique URLs scanned per day  |  VT still has quota?
  -----------------------------|---------------------
  Up to 500 unique URLs/day   |  Yes, comfortably
  500–600 unique URLs/day     |  VT may run out (others still work)
  600+ unique URLs/day        |  VT unavailable, ML + rules still run

  NOTE: Even without VT quota, the bot still gives you:
    ✅ ML model prediction (trained on 235k real URLs)
    ✅ Google Safe Browsing check
    ✅ Rule-based analysis
    ✅ A risk score and recommendation
  It just notes "VT unavailable" in the report.


----------------------------------------------------------------
TIPS TO GET THE MOST OUT OF IT
----------------------------------------------------------------

1. DON'T SCAN THE SAME URL TWICE
   Results are cached for 24 hours. Scanning google.com
   10 times in a row burns 0 extra API calls after the first.

2. SHARE RESULTS INSTEAD OF SCANNING SEPARATELY
   If a friend wants to check the same suspicious link,
   just forward the bot's report to them instead of having
   them scan it again.

3. SCAN ONLY WHAT'S GENUINELY SUSPICIOUS
   Don't test the bot with google.com, youtube.com, etc.
   Save quota for links you actually received from strangers
   or that look off (random characters, unfamiliar domains).

4. BEST TIMES TO SCAN
   The VT quota resets every 24 hours (midnight UTC = 5:30 AM IST).
   If quota runs out in the evening, try again next morning.

5. WHAT TO SEND THE BOT
   ✅ Links from WhatsApp forwards you didn't ask for
   ✅ URLs in suspicious emails/SMS
   ✅ Short links (bit.ly, tinyurl) before clicking
   ✅ Links from Instagram DMs/stories from strangers
   ✅ "You won a prize" type links
   ❌ Don't waste quota on: amazon.com, google.com, youtube.com


----------------------------------------------------------------
UNDERSTANDING YOUR RISK SCORE
----------------------------------------------------------------

  0  – 20   ✅  SAFE           — Go ahead, looks clean
  21 – 40   🟡  MODERATE RISK  — Proceed with caution
  41 – 65   🟠  SUSPICIOUS     — Avoid if possible
  66 – 80   🔴  DANGEROUS      — Do not open
  81 – 100  🚨  CRITICAL       — Definitely malicious


----------------------------------------------------------------
FOR YOUR FRIENDS — SHARING THE BOT
----------------------------------------------------------------

Just share your bot's Telegram username with them.
Each person gets their own 10-second cooldown,
but everyone shares the same daily API quota.

If you expect heavy usage (10+ active users daily),
the VirusTotal 500/day limit will be the first to run out.
The ML model and Safe Browsing will still keep working.

Recommended for a friend group:
  → Up to ~10 friends, 5-10 scans each per day = fine
  → More than that? Start by scanning only truly suspicious links


----------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT THE BOT CANNOT DO
----------------------------------------------------------------

  ✗ It cannot open/visit the URL to check the page content
    (that would require a headless browser, not on free tier)
  ✗ It cannot scan QR codes (scan QR → get URL → paste URL)
  ✗ It cannot check attachments (PDFs, images, files)
  ✗ It cannot guarantee 100% accuracy — always use judgment
    alongside the score, especially for moderate-risk URLs
